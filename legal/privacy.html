RiskWise is an educational options-risk check app. This policy covers the current TestFlight beta of RiskWise.

Information RiskWise Uses

RiskWise may store account information, profile preferences, risk rules, saved checks, chat history, uploaded-file metadata, and user-confirmed contract details. These are used to personalize risk explanations and keep the app useful across sessions.

RiskWise does not require brokerage credentials for the current beta. If broker-connection features are added later, they will use a separate consent flow and a separate privacy review.

Market and AI Data

RiskWise may send non-sensitive trade-check context to its backend so it can calculate risk metrics and generate educational explanations. If hosted AI providers are configured, the backend may send compact prompts containing the user question, the selected check, and relevant profile preferences. RiskWise does not send passwords, payment details, or unnecessary personal identifiers to AI providers.

Uploads

If you upload a screenshot or file, RiskWise stores only the metadata and the extracted or confirmed trade context needed for analysis, unless a future version explicitly asks to store the original file.

Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your RiskWise account data, including saved checks, chat history, profile settings, and uploaded-context metadata, from within the app or by contacting us.

Educational Use

RiskWise is educational decision-support software. It does not execute trades, provide financial advice, or guarantee investment outcomes.
